# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Fluxora a respectful, constructive experience for everyone regardless of background, experience level, identity, or platform background.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus reviews and discussions on technical clarity, correctness, and maintainability.
- Accept constructive feedback and provide specific, actionable suggestions on pull requests and issues.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time by verifying reproduction steps on macOS before filing bug reports.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, derogatory comments, or trolling in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Conduct inappropriate in a professional software project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close comments, lock threads, or remove contributors who repeatedly violate these standards. Report concerns directly to the repository owner via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SahilMore06/Fluxora/issues).
